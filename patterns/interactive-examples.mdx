Use these local simulations to compare what happens at a RevoEngine boundary. Start with a guard rejection, a shared concurrency key, or a receiver that loses its response after applying an effect. The scenarios use synthetic data, and their playback ticks are illustrative.

Use **Next step** to inspect a boundary. **Play** advances the example at a steady pace. Select an earlier stage in a walkthrough or use the example’s reset controls to revisit a result. With reduced motion, use manual steps.

Exact fields, defaults, limits and failure semantics remain in the guide beside each demonstration and in [Reference](/low-code/reference/index). Historical release notes and legal terms are not simulations.
